Attorney & CPA briefing sheet

Purpose: evaluate a future trust transfer of property A alongside already-owned property B. Decision, date and rationale: not yet determined. Keep private figures off this sheet if shared publicly.

Decisions requested

Compare a same-tax-year sale, live, rent, live-then-sell, rent-then-sell and sale with staged investing at matching dates and horizons. No recommendation until evidence is verified.

Attorney: trust, property & local rules

Confirm trustee powers, title, debts, deadlines, Proposition 19 eligibility and actual long-term occupancy, A tax-rate area, transfer/recording costs, B leases and permits, and whether a like-kind exchange is wanted before sale. BOE ↗ · SF Rent Board ↗

CPA: estate, basis & cash flow

Review private inventory, prior taxable gifts, portability and estate liquidity; obtain written date-of-death appraisal. Model basis, selling costs, income and estate taxes, depreciation/recapture, any eligible home-sale exclusion and the California 1% surtax only above its threshold. IRS Form 706 ↗ · FTB ↗

Evidence and policy

Obtain independent sales and rent comparables, insurance quotes and five-year capital plans for both properties. Agree on reserves, risk limits and investment staging; include reinvestment risk and record a decision and review date privately.
