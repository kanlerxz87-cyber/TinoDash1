Model mixed paths, not only the three pure choices — same transfer dates & horizons, with taxes, debt, occupancy, capital & liquidity

Pure choices vs mixed paths (Live, Rent, Sell, Live→Sell, Rent→Sell, Phased)

All paths start at same inheritance date (example 2040) and are measured at identical 10/20/30/50-yr horizons. Each includes: federal + CA income tax on rents/gains (incl. 1% MHS over $1M, NIIT 3.8% where thresholds met), property tax (Prop 13 cap vs reset vs Prop 19 carry), HOA/debt (none assumed — verify), occupancy, 5-yr capital, insurance/utilities, vacancy, brokerage/transfer tax on exit, and liquidity scoring. Imputed rent is an explicit input; §121 exclusion toggles on for Live→Sell.

Imputed rent input — for living paths

This is not market rent for A — it is the owner’s saved rent. $5,200/mo = $62,400/yr tax-free housing benefit. Use Zillow/Redfin comparables for 95014: currently $4,800–$7,500/mo for 3–4BR; the model defaults mid-range. NEEDS COMPS — see Questions

§121: Exclude $250k (single) / $500k (MFJ) of gain if owned+used as principal residence 2 of last 5 yr. Source: IRS Pub 523, 26 USC §121. Must meet use test — document move-in.

Same-year sale — decide this before anything else

Model a sale in the same tax year as the inheritance

With step-up basis to DOD market value (IRC §1014), a sale within the same tax year produces near-zero capital gain — only post-DOD appreciation to sale date is taxed. This eliminates the need to forecast 10-yr appreciation for the Sell path and avoids the Prop 13 reset carry entirely.

If you live in A → no depreciation, but §121 exclusion available. If you rent A → depreciation then recapture. Mixed path #4 (live 2 yr then sell) avoids recapture and captures exclusion — often the mathematical sweet spot if occupancy is feasible.
