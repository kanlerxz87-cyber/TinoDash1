Inheritance process — step by step

How a California house held in a trust passes to an heir, and every deadline that follows. Deadlines are shown first as a timeline, then in procedural order.

Deadline timeline

  • Death of the decedent — date-of-death value fixed here. Obtain a written valuation of Property A as of this date: it sets income-tax basis (§1014) and the Prop 19 market value.

    Source: IRS Pub. 544 · BOE Prop 19

  • Immediately — Trustee confirms authority: certify the trust (Probate Code §18100.5), obtain an EIN for the trust, notify insurers and the county of the change.

    CA Prob. Code §18100.5

  • Within 1 year of the transfer — Prop 19 occupancy + homeowner exemption. The heir must occupy A as a principal residence; file BOE-19-P (parent-child exclusion) and BOE-266 (homeowner exemption) with the Santa Clara County Assessor.

    BOE Prop 19 · BOE-19-P · BOE-266

  • 9 months after death (+6-month extension) — Federal Form 706 if the estate exceeds the exclusion. Also elect portability (DSUE) here if a spouse survives — it is lost forever if the 706 is not timely filed.

    IRS Form 706 · Instructions

  • With Form 706 (or 30 days after) — Form 8971 / Schedule A reporting final estate values to beneficiaries (basis consistency rules).

    IRS Form 8971

  • Within 3 years (or before sale to a third party) — BOE-19-P hard deadline. Miss it and the property is reassessed at full market value.

    BOE Prop 19 FAQ

  • April 15 of the year after death — final Form 1040 for the decedent; trust Form 1041 if the trust has income; CA Form 541 for the trust.

    IRS Form 1041 · FTB forms

  • If A is rented — annually — Schedule E, depreciation (27.5-yr), CA Form 540 with rental income. City/county rental registration and rent-control compliance as applicable.

    IRS Pub. 527

  • If A is sold — within 45/180 days for a 1031 — identify replacement property within 45 days, close within 180 days. Requires a qualified intermediary arranged before closing.

    IRS Form 8824

  • Each year — assessor's notice of assessed value arrives; verify the Prop 13/19 calculation and protest by the county deadline if wrong.

    BOE Prop 13 FAQ · County assessment appeals board

Step-by-step process

Phase 1 · Before death (now → 2040–2050)

  1. Read the trust instrument. Confirm it is a revocable living trust and that A is titled in the trust — otherwise A passes through probate.
  2. Confirm the trustee and successor trustee, and whether the trust splits into sub-trusts at death.
  3. Document portability and prior taxable gifts now (Form 709 history). Ask the question while the decedent can answer it — see Q-09.
  4. Write the investment policy for sale proceeds and the estate liquidity plan before any money moves (Q-10, Q-12).
  5. Agree in writing what would make the household choose each path — pre-commit criteria.
  6. Keep the private estate inventory in a secured, offline location — never in this repository.

Phase 2 · At death — the first 90 days

  1. Obtain 10–15 certified death certificates.
  2. Trustee accepts, obtains an EIN (Form SS-4), opens trust banking.
  3. Order the date-of-death appraisal for A (and for B, for estate-value purposes if applicable).
  4. Notify the property insurer — coverage must continue; vacancy terms differ from owner-occupied terms.
  5. Notify the Santa Clara County Assessor of the change of ownership; the assessor issues a supplemental or new assessment.
  6. Secure the property, change locks, redirect mail, stop the decedent's utilities where appropriate.
  7. Inventory all assets, including cash and securities — this is what decides whether the federal exclusion is crossed.

Phase 3 · The decision window (months 1–12)

  1. Test the same-year sale first (the model's default comparison). With step-up basis, gain is approximately zero; every month of delay adds ordinary gain and, if rented, depreciation recapture.
  2. Validate Prop 19 eligibility: will the heir actually live there? If no, the exclusion is not available and the rent path carries full market assessment.
  3. Choose and document the path, with the date and the reason, next to the decision — a written record for future readers.
  4. If selling: list, and price transfer tax and recording fees into the proceeds. If a 1031 is wanted, set up the qualified intermediary before closing.
  5. If renting: obtain three independent rent opinions, verify each lease and rent-control status, and file the rental registration.
  6. If living in it: move in within the year, file BOE-19-P and BOE-266, and keep proof of occupancy.

Phase 4 · Ongoing (year 2 onward)

  1. Recurring: property tax (two installments — verify the county's dates), insurance, utilities, maintenance per the five-year plan.
  2. Annual: income tax with the correct Schedule E or §121 treatment; review the assessment notice.
  3. Annual: re-run the model with updated inputs and record whether the recommendation moved.
  4. Annual: re-check federal/state/local law changes and the proposals tracker.
