Discovery plan — suggestions to close the gaps

  1. One meeting, three documents. Take the Briefing Sheet to the attorney and CPA in a single session; ask them to answer Q-03, Q-09, Q-10, Q-11, Q-14 in writing.
  2. Order the county documents this month. Tax bills for A and B, and preliminary title reports — they resolve three of the ten blocking questions.
  3. Commission the appraisals early. An appraisal dated to the date of death is only credible if ordered promptly; a later "retrospective" appraisal is weaker evidence for the IRS.
  4. Get three rent opinions and two insurance quotes in the same month, so the ranges are comparable and dated.
  5. Use the model's sensitivity range rather than a single number whenever a value is still blank; the Model page is built for exactly that.
  6. Record every answer with a date next to the item, so a future reader can see what was known when the decision was made.
