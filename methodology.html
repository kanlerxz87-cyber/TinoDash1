Privacy-first — personal information is top priority, highest urgency

How privacy is enforced

  • Anonymized placeholders: Property A (Cupertino 00000) and Property B (SF 00000) — no street addresses, no APNs, no owner names.
  • Rounded to $10: All figures rounded per request; exact county records not reproduced verbatim to prevent search de-anonymization.
  • Internal-only data: Any personal info discovered during research is for internal calculation only and never appears on the website (per instruction). Private inventory is a local encrypted file, not hosted, not committed (.gitignored).
  • No manual input required: Site works without user-uploaded personal data; what-if controls are local-browser only and not persisted to server.
