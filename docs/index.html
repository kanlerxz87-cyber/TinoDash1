What Would Change the Answer?

  • Long-Term Occupancy of Property A + High Imputed Rent: If the household genuinely plans to live in Property A for 10+ years, claiming Prop 19 saves ~$20,040/yr in property tax (at 2045) and unlocks renting Property B's 3rd unit, narrowing the gap with selling.
  • Immediate Post-Inheritance Market Crash (1929 / 2008): Lump-sum equity reinvestment suffers severe sequence-of-returns risk. Parking sale proceeds in 3M–2Y Treasuries for 24–36 months (Phased Investing) softens crash drawdowns — see Black Swan & Phased Investing (with reinvestment risk shown beside the headline).
  • Estate Exceeding Federal Exclusion ($15,000,000 single / $30,000,000 married with portability in 2026): A 40% federal estate tax due within 9 months forces an estate liquidity plan and may require selling Property A regardless of long-term return projections.
  • Cupertino Market Rent Comparables: The Rent path currently ranks lowest due to full FMV reassessment and low Bay Area cap rates; independent 00000 MLS comparables (not voucher floors) are required to confirm whether Rent is viable.
  • Enacted Law Changes (2026–2050): Repeal/modification of Prop 19, reduction of the federal estate exclusion, or changes to IRC § 1014 step-up in basis would materially shift the ranking.

Pre-Commitment Decision Rules (Agreed Before Figures Arrive)

To prevent reverse-engineering a choice from a single projection, agree in writing on these triggers before the transfer date:

  1. Choose Sell Immediate (Pure 3) or Sell + Phased Treasuries (Mixed 3) if the household does not plan to live in Property A for at least 5+ years, OR if the estate needs liquidity for federal estate tax (Form 706 due in 9 months).
  2. Choose Live in Property A (Pure 1) or Live 5 Yrs then Sell (Mixed 1) ONLY if the heir will occupy Property A as their primary residence within 1 year (qualifying for Prop 19) and wants the Cupertino lifestyle/schools while retaining Property B as a 3-unit income asset.
  3. Choose Rent Property A (Pure 2) or Rent then 1031 (Mixed 2) ONLY if independent 00000 rental comparables show a net cap rate competitive with after-tax Treasuries/equities after full FMV reassessment and landlord insurance.
