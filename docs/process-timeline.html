Step-by-Step Inheritance Process, Deadlines Timeline & 5-Year Capital Plan

Operational roadmap for trust administration, statutory deadlines, building maintenance, and private estate inventory.

Visual Deadlines Timeline (From Date of Death = Day 0)

Days 1–30 · Immediate Trust Administration
Secure Property A & Notify Insurer: Obtain certified death certificates, notify homeowner's insurance carrier (vacancy/trustee endorsement), and maintain water/solar accounts.
Days 30–60 · Written Date-of-Death Valuation
Order Certified Appraisal: Obtain an independent written date-of-death appraisal of Property A to establish the IRC § 1014 stepped-up income-tax basis.
Within 150 Days · Santa Clara County Assessor Notice
File Change in Ownership Statement (Death of Real Property Owner): File BOE-502-D with the Santa Clara County Assessor within 150 days of death to avoid penalties.
Within 9 Months · Federal Estate Tax (IRS Form 706)
File Form 706 & Pay Any Estate Tax: Due 9 months after death (6-month extension available via Form 4768, but tax payment is due at 9 months). Required if gross estate + prior taxable gifts exceed exclusion OR to elect portability (DSUE).
Within 12 Months (1 Year) · Proposition 19 Hard Deadline
Move In & File Homeowners' Exemption (if claiming Prop 19): Heir must occupy Property A as principal residence AND file BOE-19-P + Homeowners' Exemption within 1 year of transfer.
Same Tax Year · Same-Year Sale Benchmark
Execute Sale (if Sell Path chosen): Selling within the same tax year as death minimizes post-death appreciation/tax friction and locks in § 1014 basis.

Private Estate Inventory & Privacy Containment Checklist (Keep Offline)

Keep this inventory in an encrypted offline vault — never commit to GitHub:

  • Exact street addresses, APNs (Assessor's Parcel Numbers), and Santa Clara County Tax Rate Area (TRA) code.
  • Trust instrument, successor trustee contact, and beneficiary shares.
  • Full balance sheet of liquid cash, brokerage accounts, retirement accounts, and life insurance to test against the $15M (2026) federal estate exclusion.
  • History of prior taxable gifts (Form 709) and any predeceased spouse Form 706 portability election (DSUE).
  • Property B's signed leases, rent roll, security deposits, and SF Rent Board registration status.
