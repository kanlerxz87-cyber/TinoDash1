Black Swan Economic Conditions & Phased-Investing Stress Test

Growth and inflation are never uniform over 50 years. This section tests 5 historical crisis regimes occurring immediately after inheriting Property A (2045 transfer, 20-year horizon), comparing Immediate Lump-Sum Equities against Phased-Investing (parking sale proceeds in 3M–2Y Treasuries for 24–36 months before phasing into stocks).

Headline + Reinvestment Risk Side-by-Side: Phased-Investing in 3M–2Y Treasuries (which are also exempt from California state income tax) dramatically cushions severe multi-year crash entries like 1929 and 2000. However, Reinvestment Risk is real: in V-shaped crashes (2008, 2022) or normal bull markets, maturing short-term Treasuries roll over at lower Fed-cut yields and miss early equity rebounds (base-case opportunity drag: ~$1,221,540 over 20 yrs).

Recommended Investment Policy Statement (IPS) Guardrail

  • Write the IPS before the money arrives: Pre-specify whether sale proceeds will be invested via lump-sum (higher expected value) or a 24–36 month Treasury ladder (lower regret/drawdown).
  • Use 3M–2Y U.S. Treasuries (not long-duration bonds): Avoids 2022-style duration losses and is exempt from California's 12.3%–13.3% state income tax.
  • Review extreme scenarios annually: Re-run stress tests each year as transfer dates approach and interest-rate regimes shift.
