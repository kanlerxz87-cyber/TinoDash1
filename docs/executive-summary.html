What we can and cannot conclude

Can conclude now

  • The decision is highly sensitive to Prop 19 eligibility, basis/valuation, and realistic operating assumptions.
  • A phased investing path (Treasury/short-duration ladder before staged equity entry) is a useful stress-test against 1929/2008-style entry timing.
  • Property B ownership creates a live/rent hybrid option and affects liquidity/risk; it must be modeled alongside Property A.
  • No final recommendation is defensible without independent valuations, trust/estate documents, and local legal/tax review.

Cannot conclude now

  • Whether the heir will qualify for Prop 19 exclusion at transfer.
  • Actual after-tax sale proceeds, because sale date, basis, depreciation, fees, and tax state are unknown.
  • Whether renting Property A beats selling, because market rent comps and capex are unverified.
  • Whether estate will owe federal estate tax, because total estate value, gifts, portability, and 2040–2050 law are unknown.
