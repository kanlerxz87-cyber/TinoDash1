TinoDash1 Estate Decision Dashboard

Decision Framework

Structured process for making the Property A decision while preserving optionality and minimizing avoidable tax/legal/sequence-risk mistakes.

Decision principles

  • Privacy first: no personal identifiers in published outputs; round published amounts to nearest $10.
  • Evidence before ranking: no path is "best" until Prop 19 eligibility, valuations, rent comps, trust terms, and tax basis are verified.
  • Compare like-for-like: same transfer date, horizon, tax treatment, occupancy, capex, debt, and liquidity assumptions.
  • Model ranges, not point estimates: every key assumption gets low/base/high and sensitivity.
  • Sequence risk matters: selling and investing immediately is not equivalent to phased entry.
  • Document decisions: record what was decided, when, why, and what would change it.
