Stress Testing

Black Swan & Real-World Economic Scenarios

Growth and inflation are not consistent over 50 years. This page stress-tests Property A paths against crash entry, inflation spikes, rate shocks, liquidity crunches, and phased investing. It is a scenario lab, not a forecast.

Reinvestment risk headline: Selling Property A and investing proceeds concentrates risk at the entry date. A lump-sum investment made immediately before a major drawdown can underperform for years even if long-run averages look attractive.

Phased Investing Path (Required Test)

Sell → Park in 3M–2Y Treasuries for 24–36 months → Phase into stocks

Reinvestment risk: Phasing reduces bad-entry-date risk, but creates cash drag/reinvestment risk if markets rise early and Treasury yields fall when rolled.

0. Pre-sale (before listing)Write investment policy statement (IPS): target allocation, DCA schedule, max lump sum, liquidity reserve, tax reserve, rebalancing rules.Prevents emotional timing decisions after proceeds arrive.No policy = behavior risk.IPS, tax estimate, emergency/liquidity reserve.

Annual Review Triggers

  • Annually: Re-run projections with updated FMV, rent, insurance, tax rates, assessed value, and portfolio value.
  • On law change: Recheck IRS/BOE/FTB/Santa Clara/SF official updates; distinguish enacted vs proposed.
  • On life change: Occupancy, marriage, heirs, liquidity needs, disability, or desire to move.
  • On market shock: Do not abandon IPS impulsively; review against pre-committed rules.
