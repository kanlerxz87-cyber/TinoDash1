C. Information-Gathering Plan

Collect privately (do not commit to repo)

  • Trust/deed/title/loan/insurance documents.
  • Date-of-death appraisal and estate inventory.
  • Lease agreements, rent roll, tenant notices, SF compliance docs.
  • 12-24 months utility bills, tax bills, repair invoices.
  • Heir tax returns summary (private, CPA only).

Obtain from official/public/professional sources

  • Santa Clara County Assessor/Clerk-Recorder for TRA, transfer/recording rules.
  • SF Rent Board for rent-control/OMI rules.
  • IRS/FTB/BOE for current tax rules.
  • Licensed appraiser and rent comp analyst.
  • Insurance broker quotes for primary residence vs landlord policy.

Use this page as the pre-decision gate. No high-impact decision should be made with missing high-impact facts.
