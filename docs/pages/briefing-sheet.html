Assets & Goal

  • Property A: Cupertino, Santa Clara County, CA. To be inherited by trust ~2040-2050. Est. ~$3,100,000 (Nov 2026, unverified, rounded). Low assessed base; Prop 19 reassessment risk.
  • Property B: San Francisco, CA. Already owned by same prospective inheritor. Est. ~$1,800,000. 3 units: 2 rented (~$7,150/mo total, unverified), 1 owner-occupiable.
  • Liquid estate: "Considerable" amount, unknown. May affect federal estate tax/liquidity.
  • Goal: Maximize after-tax, risk-adjusted outcome; lower costs; decide live/rent/sell/mixed for Property A.

What Would Change the Answer

  • Prop 19: Primary-residence occupancy + filings within deadlines can preserve partial base; otherwise reassessment likely.
  • Date-of-death FMV & basis: Drives gain, estate tax, and cap math.
  • Estate size vs $15M federal exclusion (2026): Portability/prior gifts matter.
  • Sale timing: Same tax year can trigger CA 1% over $1M + NIIT/recapture consequences.
  • Actual rent/capex/insurance: Rent path currently speculative.
  • Liquidity: Forced-sale risk overrides long-run optimization.
