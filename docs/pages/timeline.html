Deadlines Timeline

Timeline view of key decisions and deadlines. Dates below are relative to transfer/date of death because actual future dates are unknown. Confirm every deadline with counsel and official forms.

Deadline risk: Missing Prop 19 or tax deadlines can permanently increase taxes. Use this as a checklist, not legal advice.
