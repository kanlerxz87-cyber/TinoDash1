1) Separate the Three Tax Layers (Do Not Conflate)

Estate tax, income-tax basis, and property-tax assessment are different systems
LayerWhat it taxes/measuresKey rule (official)What to verify privatelySource
Federal estate/gift tax Transfer of taxable estate at death (plus adjusted taxable gifts/specific exemption as applicable). 2026 basic exclusion amount is $15,000,000 per individual; portability election may allow surviving spouse to use decedent's unused exclusion (DSUE) if timely elected on Form 706. Top marginal rate is 40% on amounts above exclusion per IRS framework. Total estate value, prior taxable gifts, marital/charitable deductions, portability, trust structure, citizenship/residency, liquidity. IRS: Estate Tax; IRS: What's New - Estate and Gift Tax
Income tax / basis Gain/loss on later sale; depreciation recapture; rental income; capital gains. Inherited property generally receives a basis adjustment under IRC §1014 (often "step-up" or "step-down" to fair market value at date of death, subject to applicable rules/elections). California generally conforms in many areas but has its own tax treatment; capital gains are taxed as ordinary income for CA state income tax rates. Date-of-death FMV appraisal; alternate valuation election (if estate qualifies/elects); improvements; depreciation taken/allowed after inheritance; holding period; sale costs. IRS Pub. 551 (Basis of Assets); IRS Topic 703; FTB 2025 Form 540 Booklet
Property tax assessment (CA) Assessed value for property tax under Prop 13/19 rules. Change in ownership generally triggers reassessment to current market value unless an exclusion applies. Prop 19 allows limited parent-child exclusion for a family home/farm if transferee claims it as primary residence and timely files required claims; value cap applies. Whether Property A was parent's primary residence; whether heir will occupy within 1 year; homeowner exemption filing; BOE-19-P filing; factored base year value; market value at transfer. CA BOE: Proposition 19; Cal. Rev. & Tax Code §63.2

2) California Proposition 19 (Parent-Child) - Critical for Property A

High consequence: If Property A is inherited but not used as the heir's primary residence under Prop 19 rules, reassessment to market value can materially increase annual property tax. Do not assume exclusion applies.
