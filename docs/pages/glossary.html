Glossary

Plain-language definitions for terms used in this dashboard. Definitions are educational summaries; legal/tax meaning depends on statute and facts.

TermPlain meaningWhy it matters here
Adjusted taxable giftsTaxable gifts made during life that are added back for federal estate tax calculation purposes.Can reduce remaining federal exclusion at death.
Assessed value (Prop 13)The value used to calculate California property tax, generally based on acquisition/base year value with limited annual increases, unless reassessed.Property A has a low assessed value relative to market value; reassessment risk is large.
Basis / stepped-up basisBasis is the tax cost used to calculate gain/loss. Inherited assets often receive a basis equal to fair market value at date of death (IRC §1014), subject to rules.Affects capital gains if Property A is sold.
Behavioral Health Services Tax (CA)Additional 1% California tax on taxable income above $1,000,000 (formerly Mental Health Services Tax).A large one-year sale gain can trigger it.
BOE-19-PCalifornia form used to claim parent-child reassessment exclusion under Proposition 19.Required for Prop 19 exclusion claim; deadline-sensitive.
CapExCapital expenditures: major repairs/replacements (roof, HVAC, plumbing, seismic, etc.).Must be reserved for both properties; underestimating capex distorts returns.
DSUE / portabilityDeceased Spousal Unused Exclusion; a surviving spouse may use a decedent's unused federal estate exclusion if properly elected on a timely Form 706.Relevant if estate planning involves a spouse and large assets.
Factored Base Year Value (FBYV)Prop 13 assessed/base value adjusted by annual inflation factor (generally up to 2%).Used in Prop 19 cap calculation.
Imputed rentThe economic value of living in a home you own instead of paying market rent.Needed to compare "live in Property A" fairly against rent/sell.
Like-kind exchange (1031)Tax deferral for qualifying real property exchanged for like-kind investment/business real property, with strict rules.Possible only if Property A is held for investment/business use; not for personal residence.
Prop 13California constitutional rule limiting base property tax rate and assessed value growth, with reassessment on change of ownership unless excluded.Explains low assessed values and reassessment stakes.
Prop 19California rule changing parent-child and other property tax transfer rules; parent-child exclusion now limited to primary residence/family farm with conditions and value cap.Central to Property A decision.
Reinvestment riskRisk that proceeds from a sale cannot be reinvested at expected returns, or are invested at a bad time (sequence-of-returns risk).Major risk in sell-and-invest path; phased Treasury parking addresses it.
Sequence-of-returns riskRisk that poor returns occur early when portfolio is large or withdrawals begin, causing lasting damage.Relevant if selling near a downturn or drawing from proceeds.
Step-up in basisAdjustment of inherited asset's tax basis to FMV at date of death (generally), reducing built-in gain.Can reduce capital gains if sold soon after inheritance.
TRA (Tax Rate Area)Geographic area determining which property tax rates/assessments apply.Needed for accurate Property A tax estimate; private parcel lookup required.
TrustLegal arrangement where trustee holds assets for beneficiaries under trust terms.User expects inheritance by trust around 2040-2050; trust terms control process.

Educational glossary only. Confirm definitions against official statutes/instructions for your facts.
