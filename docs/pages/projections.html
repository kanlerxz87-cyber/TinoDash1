Same-Tax-Year Sale & §121 / Prop 19 Notes

Sale in same tax year as inheritance

If Property A is sold in the same tax year as the date-of-death/inheritance period, model the combined income: any gain above stepped-up basis, depreciation recapture (if rented before sale), NIIT, CA ordinary income/capital gain treatment, and the 1% Behavioral Health Services Tax on taxable income above $1,000,000. Do not assume zero tax just because of step-up.

Model switch: "Sell in same tax year" increases caution flag and recommends CPA scenario run before listing.

Living then sell (§121) and Prop 19 carry-forward

A living-then-sell path may qualify for §121 exclusion only if ownership/use tests are met; inherited-property rules are fact-specific. If Prop 19 exclusion is obtained, the excluded base carries forward while eligibility continues; losing primary-residence status can trigger reassessment. Model both: property-tax savings during occupancy and potential tax on later sale.
