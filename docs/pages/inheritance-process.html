How the Inheritance Property Process Would Look (Step by Step)

A general California trust-inheritance roadmap for Property A. Actual steps depend on trust terms, title, debts, and whether probate/court involvement is required. Confirm with a California estate attorney.

General information only. Trust administration is fact-specific. This is not legal advice.

Property A Carrying-Cost Checklist (Post-Inheritance)

Recurring

  • Property tax (depends on reassessment/Prop 19)
  • Homeowners/landlord insurance
  • Water, electricity/gas, sewer, trash
  • Landscaping/pest/HOA (if any)
  • Property management (if rented)
  • Income tax on rent/net gains

Non-recurring / reserve

  • Roof/HVAC/plumbing/electrical
  • Seismic/foundation/drainage (as applicable)
  • Appliance replacement
  • Vacancy and tenant turnover
  • Legal/compliance costs
  • Capital improvements

General roadmap only. Confirm with CA estate attorney/CPA.
