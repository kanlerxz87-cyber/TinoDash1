Glossary

Plain-language definitions for terms used in the dashboard. Definitions are simplified; verify legal/tax meaning with official sources or counsel.

TermPlain-language definitionWhy it matters hereSource
Prop 13California constitutional rule generally limiting assessed value increases and base property tax rate structure.Explains why Property A/B assessed values are far below market estimates.CA BOE Property Tax
Prop 19California rule changing parent-child property tax exclusion and base value transfers; primary-residence conditions apply.Major factor for Property A after inheritance.CA BOE Prop 19
Factored base year valueAssessed value under Prop 13 adjusted by allowed annual factors (generally up to 2%/yr) until reassessment event.Used in Prop 19 cap calculation.CA BOE
Step-up in basisAdjustment of inherited asset's income-tax basis generally to fair market value at date of death (fact-specific).Affects capital gains if Property A is sold.IRS Pub 551
Portability (DSUE)Election allowing surviving spouse to use deceased spouse's unused federal estate tax exclusion.Can prevent wasted exemption; requires timely Form 706.IRS Form 706 Instructions
Form 706Federal estate (and GST) tax return; also used to elect portability in many cases.Filing may be needed even when no estate tax is due.IRS Form 706
1031 exchangeLike-kind exchange allowing deferral of gain on qualifying real property held for investment/business.Possible only for qualifying paths; strict rules.IRS
Sequence-of-returns riskRisk that poor returns early in a withdrawal/investment period harm long-term outcomes.Relevant when selling Property A and investing proceeds.Investor.gov Glossary
DCADollar-cost averaging: investing a fixed amount over time rather than all at once.Used in phased investing path.Investor.gov
Treasury ladderHolding Treasuries with staggered maturities to manage liquidity and reinvestment timing.Parking bucket for 24-36 month phased plan.TreasuryDirect
