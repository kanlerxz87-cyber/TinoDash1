Glossary of Terms

Plain-English definitions of tax, real estate, and investment terms used across TinoDash1.

Property Tax Terms

  • Factored Base Year Value (FBYV): A property's Prop 13 assessed value (original base value increased by at most 2% per year).
  • Proposition 19 (CA): Allows a parent-to-child transfer of a family home to keep its FBYV + an indexed allowance ($1,058,000 in 2025–2027) ONLY if the child moves in within 1 year as their primary residence.
  • Tax Rate Area (TRA): The specific geographic tax district in a California county that determines the 1% Prop 13 base rate plus local voter-approved bonds.
  • Documentary Transfer Tax: One-time tax paid when real estate is sold ($1.10 per $1,000 in Santa Clara County; $0 city tax in Cupertino).

Estate & Income Tax Terms

  • IRC § 1014 Step-Up in Basis: Resets an inherited asset's cost basis to its Fair Market Value on the date of death, eliminating prior unrealized capital gains.
  • Portability (DSUE): Deceased Spousal Unused Exclusion — allows a surviving spouse to carry over a deceased spouse's unused federal estate tax exclusion via Form 706.
  • IRC § 121 Exclusion: Excludes up to $250,000 (Single) or $500,000 (MFJ) of capital gain on the sale of a primary residence owned and lived in for 2 of the last 5 years.
  • Unrecaptured § 1250 Gain: Federal tax (up to 25%) on previously claimed residential rental depreciation when the property is sold.
  • CA 1% MHST: California's 1% Mental Health Services Tax on California taxable income exceeding $1,000,000.

Investment & Modeling Terms

  • Imputed Rent: The implicit tax-free housing benefit of living in a home you own instead of paying rent to a landlord.
  • Phased-Investing (Treasury Staging): Parking sale proceeds in 3M–2Y U.S. Treasuries for 24–36 months and gradually moving into equities to reduce crash-entry risk.
  • Reinvestment Risk: The risk that maturing short-term Treasuries must be reinvested at lower interest rates (e.g., after Fed rate cuts) or miss early equity market rebounds.
  • Sequence-of-Returns Risk: The danger that a severe market crash occurs right after a large lump-sum investment, permanently impairing long-term compounding.

San Francisco Specific Terms

  • SF Prop H (Nov 2026): San Francisco parcel tax ($129–$400,000/parcel, inflation-adjusted for 15 years) to fund Muni operations.
  • SF Prop H (2006): San Francisco Rent Board ordinance mandating inflation-adjusted relocation payments for no-fault evictions (e.g., owner move-in).
  • IRC § 1031 Exchange: Tax-deferred swap of one investment property for another like-kind investment property, deferring capital gains and depreciation recapture.
