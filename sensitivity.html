CA Mental Health Services Tax (MHS) — 1% on income > $1M, including capital gains

CA’s 1% MHS surcharge applies to CA taxable income over $1,000,000 — this includes capital gains because CA taxes gains as regular income (no preferential fed 15/20% rate). It stacks on top of 9.3–13.3% graduated rates. Official: FTB 540 Booklet 2024 (line 35 instructions) and FTB MHS Tax page. The model’s sensitivity value uses 1% only on the portion above $1M, not entire gain.
