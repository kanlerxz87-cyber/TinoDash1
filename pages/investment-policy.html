Investment Policy Statement — Proceeds of Property A

Pre-commitment for the money this decision may release. Targets are policy, not predictions; bands are hard limits. Figures rounded to $10; reviewed annually. Not investment advice — execute through the household’s chosen fiduciary advisor.

1. Objectives (in order)

  1. Capital preservation with black-swan tolerance — survive a 50% market drawdown in the first 36 months without lifestyle or tax damage.
  2. Full liquidity — no lockups; no illiquid alternatives; ≥ 6 months expected spend in the ladder’s shortest rungs at all times.
  3. Inflation-beating growth over 10+ years — consume purchasing power, not just nominal value.
  4. Tax efficiency — CA 13.3% top ordinary / 37.1% combined capital-gain drag minimized via location (Treasury interest is CA-exempt; broad equity index ETFs minimize distributions).

3. The 24–36-month phased entry (the black-swan protocol)

  1. Close of sale (same tax year as death, §1014): 100% → Treasury ladder: 25% 3-month · 25% 6-month · 25% 1-year · 25% 2-year (yields as of 2026-11-24: 4.36 / 4.25 / 4.59 / 4.63%).
  2. Months 13–36: phase-in 1/24th of the equity target per month (or 1/36th if a 2008/1929 preset is active at IPS review); each month’s tranche buys on a fixed day (dollar-cost by construction).
  3. Reinvest matured rungs into the longest rung until phase-in begins; thereafter each maturing rung funds that month’s equity purchase.
  4. Review triggers (any one pauses phase-in 30 days, never cancels it): (i) equity drawdown > 20% from phase-in start; (ii) CPI YoY > 5% for 3 consecutive prints; (iii) Treasury 3M yield moves ±200 bp since tranche pricing; (iv) estate/tax surprise surfaces.
  5. Hard prohibitions during phase-in: no margin, no options, no individual-stock concentration > 5%, no crypto, no private placements, no “just this once” lump sums, no market-timing calls — the calendar is the signal.

4. Reinvestment risk (pre-acknowledged, in writing)

If 3M/2Y yields fall materially before maturities mature, reinvestment income falls by the same amount. On ≈ $7,400,000 parked: a 200 bp fall ≈ −$148,000/yr; a 300 bp fall ≈ −$222,000/yr; near-zero ZIRP ≈ −$322,000/yr vs the 2026-11-24 curve. The ladder is not a yield decision; it is crash insurance with a known, pre-paid premium (the drag vs staying in equities straight up). If the household cannot accept that premium, the correct move is to shorten the window to 12 months — not to skip the ladder.

5. Rebalancing & review

  • Rebalance when any sleeve drifts > 5 percentage points outside bands, or semi-annually, whichever first.
  • Annual review every January (after bills land): bands, yields, inflation print, estate exclusion indexation, rent-cap changes, Prop H bill, insurance re-quote, tax-location audit (CA-exempt Treasuries in taxable; equities in taxable for LTCG harvesting; bonds/dividends in tax-deferred where available).
  • Any deviation > 20% of a sleeve’s target requires written rationale in the Decision Log + attorney/CPA sign-off.

6. Reporting

Monthly one-pager: positions, % of target, YTD return, benchmark (60/30/10 vs a target-date index), phase-in schedule vs plan, realized gains/losses, Treasury interest harvested. Quarterly: written note to the estate file. Every change to this IPS requires all signatories.
