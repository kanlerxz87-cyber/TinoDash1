3. Step-by-Step Inheritance Process & Statutory Deadline Timeline

Pre-Death Planning (Now – Transfer Year): Verify A/B vs Survivor trust structure, check prior Form 709 taxable gifts, confirm DSUE portability on any predeceased spouse's estate, sign Household Pre-Commitment Criteria & Written Investment Policy Statement (IPS).
Day 1 – Month 2 (Administration & Appraisal): Order certified death certificates, lodge will/trust notifications (CA Probate Code §16061.7), retain qualified appraiser for Date-of-Death FMV (establishes both Form 706 & IRC §1014 basis). Separate Property A cash flow cleanly into trust estate account.
Month 6 — IRC §2032 Alternate Valuation Check: Evaluate whether estate value and estate tax BOTH fell during the 6 months post-death (Form 706 Instructions).
Month 9 — Federal Form 706 & Estate Tax Liquidity Deadline: File Form 706 and pay any 40% estate tax due (or file Form 4768 for automatic 6-month filing extension; tax payment still due at 9 mo unless §6166 closely-held deferral applies).
Within 30 Days of Form 706 — Form 8971 & Schedule A: Executor files Form 8971 locking §1014(f) consistent basis with IRS and beneficiaries.
Within 1 Year — Prop 19 Occupancy & Homeowners' Exemption (Critical!): If choosing Path 3 or 4, heir MUST physically occupy Property A as principal residence AND file Homeowners' / Disabled Veterans' Exemption + BOE-19-P (BOE Prop 19). Note: same-tax-year sale (Path 1/5) avoids holding-period rental complexity.
Year 3 — Prop 19 Outer Claim Window & Annual Form 3840: BOE-19-P outer 3-year filing window (retroactive relief strictly limited after 3 yrs). Out-of-state §1031 exchanges (Path 6) must file CA FTB Form 3840 annually for life until disposition.
Year 5 — Late DSUE Portability Relief (Rev. Proc. 2022-32): Final outer window to elect spousal portability on a non-taxable Form 706 if missed at Month 9.
