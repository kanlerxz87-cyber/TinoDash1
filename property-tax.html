Property tax — how it actually works in California (Prop 13 + Prop 19 + TRA)

Official step to verify TRA (do this before relying on any rate):
  1. Go to Santa Clara County Assessor Parcel Search → enter APN (private) → note Tax Rate Area (TRA) — e.g., Cupertino TRAs are in the 008-xxx, 013-xxx range. Source: SCC Assessor — Tax Information · SCC Finance — Property Tax Rates / TRA Book
  2. Open the SCC Annual TRA Rate Book (FY 2024-25 PDF) — find your TRA row — total combined rate = 1% base + voter-approved bonds/parcels. Source: SCC Property Tax Rates (official TRA publication). If link moves, search “Santa Clara County Tax Rate Book 2024-25”.
  3. For Cupertino, typical total is ~1.16–1.25% of assessed value (1% Prop 13 base + local school/community-college/water district levies). The site’s table uses 1.18% mid with low/high range.
  4. Record the TRA and total rate in your private inventory; re-check annually (levies change when bonds retire/pass).

Prop 19 — carry the allowance forward (only if heir will occupy long-term)

Claim the Prop 19 exclusion only if the heir will occupy the home long-term — then price the carry correctly

CA Prop 19 (effective 2021) eliminated old parent-child exclusion. Now the heir keeps the low assessed value only if: (1) Property A was the decedent’s principal residence, (2) heir files Homeowners’ Exemption within 1 year and makes it their principal residence, and (3) claim filed within 1 year of transfer. Otherwise assessed value resets to market at DOD. Market value above assessed + $1M (adjusted) is added back.

Carry-forward: Prop 19’s taxable value = prior assessed value + (market value – assessed value – $1M allowance, if positive, indexed). Allowance adjusts annually for CA House Price Index. See BOE Prop 19 official page and SCC Assessor Prop 19 filing.

Example carry-forward math (illustrative, 2026 allowance ~$1M+ indexed)
Prior assessed $620,000 + max(0, $2,400,000 – $620,000 – $1,000,000) = ~$2,100,040 new assessed if occupancy claimed but market exceeds allowance
If vacancy/no occupancy → assessed = $2,400,000 (full reset). Prop tax at 1.18% = $36,580/yr vs $25,000/yr with partial allowance vs $6,500/yr with full carry (if under allowance). Must verify indexed allowance at DOD year.
Decision trigger
Only claim Prop 19 if you will occupy A ≥2–3 yr and file BOE-19-P within 1 yr. Otherwise model full reset.
If you will not occupy long-term, selling same-year avoids paying elevated prop tax at all. Deadline · BOE source
Verification required: Obtain DOD appraisal; SCC Preliminary Change in Ownership Report (BOE-502-A) + Prop 19 claim; Homeowners’ Exemption (BOE-266). Missing the 1-yr window = lose the exclusion permanently. See Timeline and BOE.
