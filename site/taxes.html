Separate Estate Tax, Income-Tax Basis & Property-Tax Assessment

A common inheritance mistake is conflating three independent tax systems that operate under different rules:

1. Federal Estate Tax (Form 706)
Taxes the total net estate above the Basic Exclusion Amount ($15,000,000 per individual in 2026; $30M married with portability) at up to 40%. California has no state estate or inheritance tax.
2. Income-Tax Basis (IRC §1014)
Resets Property A's income-tax cost basis to Fair Market Value at Date of Death. Selling in the same tax year as inheritance typically produces ~$0 federal and CA capital gain.
3. CA Property-Tax Assessment (Prop 13 / Prop 19)
Reassesses Property A to FMV upon inheritance unless the heir moves in within 1 year as a primary residence (Prop 19 exclusion: FBYV + $1,044,586 allowance in 2025–2027). Move-out triggers reassessment.
