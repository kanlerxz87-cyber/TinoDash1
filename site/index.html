What Would Change the Answer?

Under baseline 2045 assumptions (15-year horizon), Sell Immediately (Same Tax Year) leads on median after-tax wealth, followed closely by Mixed: Live 4 yrs → Sell (§121) and Sell + 30-Month Treasury Staging. The recommendation flips if any of these four conditions hold:

  • Estate Exceeds Federal Exclusion ($15M+ indexed): Forces immediate liquidity planning (40% federal estate tax due in 9 months), strongly favoring a same-tax-year sale or liquidating non-RE assets first.
  • Equity Crash Near Inheritance (1929 / 2008): Phasing sale proceeds through 3M–2Y Treasuries for 24–36 months outperforms Lump-Sum Sell by $1.5M–$3.2M, though it carries bull-market reinvestment drag.
  • Heir Wants to Live in Cupertino Long-Term + Rent 3rd SF Unit: Claiming Prop 19 + tax-free imputed rent + renting Property B's 3rd unit closes most of the gap with Selling.
  • Cupertino Rent Comparables > $7,500/mo (2026$): Only if verified CMA rents materially exceed baseline ($6,000/mo) does Renting Property A become competitive after full Prop 13 reassessment.
