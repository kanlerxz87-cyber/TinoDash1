Glossary of Key Terms

Term Plain-English Definition
DSUE / PortabilityDeceased Spousal Unused Exclusion: allows a surviving spouse to inherit a predeceased spouse's unused federal estate tax exclusion if a timely Form 706 was filed.
FBYVFactored Base Year Value: a California property's Prop 13 assessed value (original base year value compounded by max 2%/yr inflation).
Imputed RentThe tax-free housing value an owner receives by living in their own home instead of paying market rent elsewhere.
IRC §1014 (Step-Up)Federal/CA rule resetting an inherited asset's income-tax cost basis to Fair Market Value at Date of Death, wiping out prior unrealized capital gains.
IRC §121 ExclusionPrimary residence capital gains exclusion ($250,000 Single / $500,000 Married Filing Jointly) if owned and occupied 2 of the last 5 years.
IRC §1031 ExchangeTax-deferred like-kind exchange of investment/rental real estate (strict 45-day identification and 180-day closing clocks).
IRC §1250 RecaptureFederal tax (up to 25% + 3.8% NIIT) on depreciation previously claimed on a rental building when sold without a §1031 exchange.
MHSA (Form 540)California Mental Health Services Tax: additional 1% state income tax on taxable income above $1,000,000 (bringing top CA rate to 13.3%).
Prop 19 AllowanceCA intergenerational exclusion cap (FBYV + $1,044,586 for 2025–2027, adjusted biennially) available ONLY if heir occupies inherited home as primary residence within 1 year.
Prop H (SF, Nov 2026)Enacted 15-year San Francisco parcel tax funding SFMTA/Muni; landlords may pass through 50% (up to ~$65/yr) to rent-controlled tenants.
Reinvestment RiskThe risk that parking sale proceeds in short-term Treasuries misses bull-market equity gains (opportunity drag) or faces lower yields when T-bills mature and roll over.
TRA (Tax Rate Area)Santa Clara County geographic code (Cupertino prefix 013) determining exact local school/voter-approved bond rates on top of the 1% Prop 13 base.
