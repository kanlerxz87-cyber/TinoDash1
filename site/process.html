Step-by-Step Inheritance Process & Deadlines Timeline

Trust administration milestones, statutory tax/assessor clocks, pre-commitment decision rules, and a dated decision log.

Critical Deadlines Timeline (From Date of Death = Day 0)

Days 1–30 · Trust Administration & Insurance Continuity
Notify successor trustee, secure certified death certificates, verify Property A homeowners insurance remains active (notify carrier of trust/estate status), and obtain EIN for irrevocable trust.
Days 30–60 · Written Date-of-Death Valuation (Appraisal)
Commission an independent licensed appraisal of Property A as of the exact Date of Death. This establishes the IRC §1014 stepped-up basis for federal/CA income taxes and Form 706 reporting.
Days 60–150 · Santa Clara County Assessor Filings
File Change in Ownership Statement — Death of Real Property Owner (BOE-502-D) within 150 days of death with the Santa Clara County Assessor (SCC Assessor).
Same Tax Year (Before Dec 31) · Model & Execute Immediate Sale Window
If selling Property A, closing in the same tax year as inheritance captures the full §1014 step-up with minimal post-death appreciation or capital gain, while allowing deduction of selling costs against the estate/trust.
Month 9 · Federal Estate Tax Return (Form 706) & Tax Payment
File IRS Form 706 and pay any federal estate tax due (or file Form 4768 for 6-month filing extension; tax payment still due at Month 9). Also required to elect DSUE Portability even if no tax is owed.
Month 12 (Within 1 Year) · Proposition 19 & Homeowners' Exemption
Only if occupying Property A long-term: Move into Property A as principal residence and file BOE-19-P + Homeowners' Exemption within 1 year of transfer (CA BOE Pub 801).
If Selling Rental via §1031 · 45-Day ID & 180-Day Close Clocks
Engage a Qualified Intermediary (QI) before closing. Identify replacement property within 45 days and close within 180 days.

Pre-Commitment Decision Criteria (Agree in Writing Before Figures Arrive)

Define these triggers in advance so the household does not reverse-engineer a decision from a single projection:

Path Household Will Choose This Path If… Primary Disqualifier
Sell Immediately (Same Tax Year) Estate needs liquidity for federal estate tax, OR heir prefers staying in Property B (SF) + diversifying out of Silicon Valley single-asset risk. Strong personal desire to live in Cupertino long-term.
Sell + Phased 24–36M Treasuries Selling Property A AND equity valuations are elevated / household prioritizes avoiding 1929/2008 Day-1 crash risk over bull-market drag. Unwillingness to accept potential bull-market opportunity lag.
Live in Property A (Prop 19) Heir commits to living in Cupertino for 5+ years AND rents out Property B's 3rd unit to offset carry costs. Short-term stay (<2 yrs) or moving out later (triggers Prop 19 reassessment).
Mixed: Live 4 yrs → Sell (§121) Heir wants to test living in Cupertino for 2–4 years while preserving IRC §121 ($250k/$500k) exclusion on post-inheritance appreciation. Need for immediate estate-tax cash at Month 9.
Rent Property A (or Rent → §1031) Independent Cupertino CMA confirms market rent >$7,500/mo (2026$) OR Prop 19 is repealed before transfer. Low net cap rate (~1.5%–2.0%) after full Prop 13 reassessment.
