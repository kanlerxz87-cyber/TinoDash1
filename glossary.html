How the menu is grouped — and what every term means

Dashboard = the 5-minute answer (Executive Summary + What would change it + Deadlines + What-ifs). Decide = the three paths plus mixed/black-swan, taxes, and timing — the core decision. Own & Operate = carrying the houses while you decide (leases, insurance, 5-yr capital). Verify = is this real? (methodology, legislation tracker, line-by-line sources, flagged unverified). Help = glossary, missing info, suggestions.
