Glossary

Plain-language definitions for every term used in this site.

TermMeaningWhere it matters here
Ad valorem taxProperty tax calculated as a percentage of assessed value.Both properties
Assessed valueThe value the county taxes, which is not market value. Capped by Prop 13.Properties
Basis (cost basis)The number subtracted from the sale price to compute taxable gain.Taxes §2
BHSTBehavioral Health Services Tax — California's 1% tax on taxable income above $1,000,000 (formerly the Mental Health Services Tax).Large sale years
BOE-19-PClaim form for the Prop 19 parent-child exclusion; due within 3 years or before sale to a third party.Deadlines
BOE-266Homeowner's property tax exemption claim; file within 1 year of occupancy.Live path
Capital gainProfit on the sale of an asset. Long-term if held over a year.Every sell path
Certification of trustA short document proving the trustee's authority without disclosing the whole trust.Steps
Change in ownershipThe event that triggers reassessment under Prop 13. Inheritance is one.Inheritance
DSUE / portabilityDeceased spousal unused exclusion — a surviving spouse can inherit the unused exclusion, but only if a timely Form 706 is filed.Estate tax
Factored base year valueThe original base year value plus allowed annual inflation adjustments (max 2%).Prop 13 / 19
Form 706Federal estate tax return; due 9 months after death, extendable 6 months.Estate tax
Form 8971Report of final estate values given to beneficiaries (basis consistency).Post-death filings
Form 1041Income tax return for the trust or estate.Trust administration
Imputed rentThe rent you would have paid to live somewhere — a real economic benefit of occupying your own home, though not taxable income.Live path
Like-kind exchange (§1031)Deferring gain by reinvesting sale proceeds into another qualifying investment property.Taxes §2
LiquidityHow quickly an asset can be turned into cash without loss.Sell vs. hold
NIITNet Investment Income Tax — 3.8% surtax on investment income above MAGI thresholds.Sale years
Prop 13California's 1978 cap: 1% base rate plus voter debt, assessed value growth capped at 2%/yr, reassessment on change of ownership.Both properties
Prop 192020 measure: parent-child exclusion limited to a primary residence the child occupies; cap = factored base + allowance.Core to the decision
Recapture (§1250)Tax on depreciation previously deducted, capped at 25% for residential real estate.Rent-then-sell
Rent controlLocal limits on rent increases and eviction grounds. In SF, applies to most units built before 13 June 1979.Property B
Reinvestment riskThe risk that cash held out of the market misses a rise — the cost of phased investing.Black Swan
Step-up in basisBasis reset to date-of-death value, erasing prior gain (§1014).Same-year sale
Supplemental assessmentAn extra property tax bill after a reassessment, covering the gap between the old and new value.Possible cause of F-07
Tax Rate Area (TRA)A geographic code that sets which bond and special assessments apply to a parcel.Flag F-02
Transfer taxA tax on recording a sale, charged by the county and sometimes the city.Sell path
Treasury ladderBills/notes bought to mature at staggered dates, so cash returns periodically at par.Phased investing
