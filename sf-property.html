What to verify before you rely on the $7,150 rent roll

CheckWhat to doSource
SF Rent Ordinance — does it cover B?SF rent control applies to multifamily built before June 13, 1979. Most Outer Sunset 2–3-unit houses built 1940s–60s are covered — even if owner-occupied. Verify via SF Rent Board — Building Status Search. If covered, annual rent increase capped at 60% of CPI (e.g., 3.6% for 2024–25, capped at 7%); vacancy partially uncontrolled but with restrictions. VERIFY COVERAGE NOWSF Rent Board · Annual Increase Limits
Current leases — written?Obtain written leases for both rented units: term, rent, deposit, pets, parking, who pays utilities, renewal, Ellis Act status. If oral/month-to-month, convert to written before 2040. CA AB 12 (2024) limits deposits to 1 mo (small landlords exempt until July 2025 — check).
