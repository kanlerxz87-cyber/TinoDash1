What Would Change the Answer? (Top 4 Decision Triggers)

1. Prop 19 Occupancy
Moving into Property A within 1 year saves ~$25k+/yr in property tax via the $1,044,586 (2025–2027 indexed) exclusion. Renting triggers full FMV reassessment.
2. Federal Estate Tax ($15M / $30M)
Under 2026 law (PL 119-21), estates above $15M single / $30M married face 40% federal tax. If liquid assets + Property A exceed this, liquidity planning is urgent.
3. Same-Year Sale vs. Hold
Selling in the inheritance year captures 100% IRC § 1014 step-up in basis ($0 capital gains). Holding and selling later without § 121 triggers up to 13.3% CA + 23.8% federal tax.
4. Phased vs. Lump-Sum Investing
Parking proceeds in 3M–2Y Treasuries for 24–36 months softens 1929/2008 crash entry risk, but carries reinvestment & inflation-drag risk in bull markets.

Executive Summary & Strategic Recommendation

Because the heir already owns Property B (San Francisco, 94122)—a 3-unit building generating ~$7,150/mo from 2 rented units while allowing owner-occupancy in the 3rd unit—retaining Property B as the primary residence preserves cash flow and avoids disrupting a low Prop 13 base (~$114,250 assessed).

Default Baseline Winner: Under standard 7.5% equity vs. 4.0% real estate assumptions, Pure Sell (Same Tax Year) or Phased Investing (24–36 mo 3M–2Y Treasuries → S&P 500) maximizes liquid after-tax wealth by capturing the full IRC § 1014 step-up in basis at death and avoiding Prop 19 reassessment on a rental. If the heir genuinely wants to relocate to Cupertino long-term, Live-In 3+ Years → Sell (IRC § 121) captures both Prop 19 and the $250k/$500k primary-residence exclusion.
