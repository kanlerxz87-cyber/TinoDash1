Glossary

IRC § 1014 (Step-Up in Basis)Resets the tax basis of inherited property to Fair Market Value at Date of Death, eliminating prior capital gains.
Prop 19 (CA)Allows parent-to-child exclusion up to $1,058,000 (2025–2027) ONLY if the heir moves in within 1 year as principal residence.
IRC § 121Excludes $250,000 (single) or $500,000 (married) of capital gain when selling a primary residence lived in 2 of the last 5 years.
IRC § 1250 RecaptureTaxes prior rental depreciation deductions upon sale (up to 25% federal + CA ordinary income).
Reinvestment RiskThe risk that cash/Treasuries earn less than equities or lose purchasing power during a 24–36 month staging window.
