Path comparison — same dates, same horizon (ranges)

PathLowMidHighNotes

Every figure is net wealth at the horizon year, so all seven paths are compared at the same date and horizon. The heir occupies exactly one dwelling in every path (symmetry rule): living in A counts A's imputed rent; every other path counts B's vacant unit's imputed rent. B's existing two rents and running costs are constant across paths, so they are held out and only differences are modelled. Hold paths are marked to market and charged the tax a sale would trigger, so they are not flattered by unrealized value. Low/High shift growth & return ±1.5pp. Debt assumed $0 on both properties — confirm no liens (see Questions Q-03). Mixed paths (live-5, rent-5, 1031) sit beside the three pure choices. Primary-residence exclusion (121) is switched on only for live-then-sell paths. Prop 19 allowance is carried forward in the live path; rent path is fully reassessed.

Break-even thresholds — the precise "what would change the answer"

Instead of adjectives, these are the thresholds at which the leading path changes, holding every other control at baseline. Each was found by bisection on the model itself, so it reproduces exactly.

ControlBaselineThresholdWhat happens on each side
Home-price growth3.5%/yr≈ 1.32%/yrAbove: living 5 years then selling leads. Below: selling in year 1 and investing leads — house appreciation no longer compensates for tying up capital.
Horizon20 years≈ 15.2 yearsLonger: hold-then-sell leads. Shorter: sell immediately leads — there is not enough time for the house's tax-advantaged growth to pay off.
Market rent for A$6,500/mo (placeholder)≈ $17,800/moThe rent path only overtakes an immediate sale if A rents for roughly 2.7× the placeholder. Until comparables exist, the rent path is not competitive on these inputs.
Housing benefit (imputed rent)OnOn / OffTurned off for all dwellings (symmetric): immediate sale leads. The ranking depends on whether the household values the housing it consumes — a genuine value judgement, not a math result.

None of these thresholds is a forecast. The point is that the answer is conditional and close to the line — a 2-percentage-point change in assumed appreciation, or a 5-year change in horizon, flips it. Record which thresholds the household considers binding before the figures arrive (pre-commit criteria).

How the model works (audit trail)

  • Home value: V0 × (1+g)^t. Assessed (Prop 13): factored base +2%/yr; Prop 19 live path adds only the excess over (factored base + cap); rent path reassesses to market.
  • Carrying costs grow at inflation; rent grows at rent-growth; net cash flows compound at the Treasury rate.
  • Sell paths deduct selling cost %, then capital-gains layers (federal LTCG + NIIT + CA ordinary; 121 exclusion on live-then-sell; 25% recapture on rent-then-sell).
  • Phased path parks proceeds at the Treasury yield for 24–36 months, then invests at the stock scenario. Reinvestment risk is shown on the Black Swan page.
  • Every input above maps to a row on Properties or Taxes with an official source or an explicit unverified flag.
