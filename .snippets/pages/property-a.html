Full reassessment applies to a non-occupying child. Market rent for a Cupertino single-family home is ≈ $6,500–$8,000/mo (≈ $78,000–$96,000/yr, UNVERIFIED — need 2 independent estimates). After tax ≈ $36,000, insurance ≈ $9,000, maintenance/capex ≈ $17,000, utilities (owner-paid or vacancy) ≈ $1,900 and 5% vacancy/management drag ≈ $4,000–$5,000, net ≈ $12,000–$27,000/yr — a ~0.4–0.9% cap rate on $3.1M versus ≈ 4.6% risk-free on the same net proceeds in 1–2-year Treasuries. Renting A only competes if (a) Prop 19 applies (⇒ the heir must live there — contradiction) or (b) FMV growth outpaces ~5%/yr for decades (speculative) or (c) the heir refuses to sell for nonfinancial reasons.

Cupertino has no local rent-stabilization/rent-control ordinance; covered units fall under AB 1482 (Civil Code §1947.12): cap = 5% + regional CPI, ≤ 10% — for Santa Clara County increases effective Aug 2026–Jul 2027 the cap is 8.6% (CA Attorney General). AB 1482 sunsets 2030-01-01 unless extended. Verify coverage (single-family owned by individuals is generally exempt if proper notice given; corporate/LLC-REIT owners lose the exemption).
