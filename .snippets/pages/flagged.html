C. “Prop H” naming caution (resolved, kept for the record)

This project tracks TWO different “Prop I / Prop H” pairs in SF Nov 2026: Prop H = Stronger Muni parcel tax (PASSED, 74.42%) and Prop I = transfer-tax revenue split (FAILED, 51.62% no). The instruction “Model Prop H with its official terms” was read as the parcel tax — the only property-tax-like measure named H on that ballot. If the owner instead meant a different measure, re-flag and re-model. Both are on Law Tracker with official results links.
