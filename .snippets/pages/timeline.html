Deadlines as a timeline · diarize everything below

Every clock that starts ticking

Day 0 = date of death (or date of trust distribution trigger). Dates in gold cost money if missed; red are hard legal cliffs; blue are advisory/annual.

  1. Day 0–60
    Death certificate (order 6–10) · successor trustee affidavit recorded · A secured & insured · trustee fee agreement in writing · trust notice to beneficiaries (Prob. Code §16061.7) ≤ 60 days
  2. Day 0–90 · CRITICAL
    Written date-of-death appraisal commissioned — feeds estate inclusion, Prop 13 reassessment, heir’s basis. Also photograph/list contents; freeze A’s thermostat, locks, alarm codes.
  3. Day 0–120
    Trust accounting opened; Form 706 readiness review: gross estate vs $15,000,000 (2026) basic exclusion ($30,000,000 couple + portability); list lifetime taxable gifts; identify incidents of ownership in life insurance (IRC §2042).
  4. Day 0 + 9 months · HARD
    IRS Form 706 due if required (estate > exclusion or electing portability); Form 4768 extends +6 months. Late portability relief may exist (Form 706 instructions) — ask the CPA. CA: no estate return (no state estate tax).
  5. Day 0 + 9 months
    Final Form 709 (decedent’s gifts) also due; Form 541 (trust) if required; first property tax installment (Nov 1) and second (Feb 1) continue as usual — calendar both.
  6. Day 0 + 1 year · HARD (occupancy paths)
    Move into A and file BOE-266 (homeowners’ exemption) — Prop 19 requires occupancy as principal residence within 1 year; later filing = exclusion only prospective; move-out = full reassessment + interest.
  7. Day 0 + 3 years · HARD (occupancy paths)
    File BOE-19-P (parent-child exclusion claim) with Santa Clara County Assessor — required within 3 years of transfer, before any third-party transfer, or within 6 months after a supplemental/escape notice.
  8. Day ~120–365
    Supplemental property-tax assessment notice arrives (reassessment event). Protest within the window printed (≈ 60 days) — check the appraisal vs the county’s value.
  9. Any year · on sale
    Selling A: Santa Clara documentary transfer tax $1.10/$1,000 (Cupertino city half-rate credited; total unchanged) + recording (≈ $15 base + pages + fees ≈ $30–$60, verify) + prorated taxes + commissions (negotiable). Seller’s SF custom contrast for B: SF transfer tax 0.75% ($1M–$5M) applies to the entire price.
  10. 2027-07-01 · NEW (Prop H)
    SF Prop H begins: multifamily parcel tax ≈ $249/yr + $0.195/sqft >5,000 sqft (cap $50,000), inflation-adjusted, through 2042-06-30; 65+ principal-residence reduction/exemption; ≤50%/$65-per-unit pass-through to rent-controlled tenants (base rent set before 6/1/2027). Verify B’s bill and building sqft.
  11. 2026-03-01 → 2027-02-28
    SF allowable rent increase for covered (pre-6/13/1979) units: +1.6% (60% Bay Area CPI). AB 1482 (SF area): +8.8% (8/2026–7/2027). Santa Clara Co.: +8.6%. Give lawful 30/60-day notices (Civ. Code §§1946.2, 827).
  12. 2030-01-01 · SUNSET
    AB 1482 sunsets unless the Legislature extends it — if extended, re-run all rent-growth assumptions; if allowed to lapse, covered-unit caps disappear (just-cause duties may persist).
  13. Every 2 years
    Prop 19 allowance re-indexed by BOE (biennial, FHFA CA HPI) — 2025-02-16→2027-02-15: $1,044,586. Carry the forward value in the model; re-pull BOE Letters to Assessors each spring.
  14. Every year (Jan 1 lien date)
    Prop 13 escalator ≤2% applied; homeowners’ exemption renewals ($7,000 assessed-value reduction, auto-continues); re-run this model with fresh Treasury curve (Sources), fresh assessed values, and Law Tracker changes.
  15. Every year · ANNUAL REVIEW
    IPS compliance: allocation drift vs policy bands; ladder reinvestment rate vs assumed 4.3–4.7%; black-swan refresh (Black Swan Lab); insurance market re-quote (CA admitted vs FAIR Plan + DIC); rent-cap recalculation; Prop H bill audit; §121 clock check (2-of-5); document the decision log (Suggestions Review Board) — with date and reason.
  16. 2040–2050 horizon
    Planning window for the actual transfer. All figures on this site are 2026-law snapshots; by 2040 the federal exclusion may be higher (indexed), AB 1482 may be extended/sunset, Prop H expires 2042-06-30. Re-verify everything at death; do not rely on this page then.
