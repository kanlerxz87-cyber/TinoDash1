Step-by-Step Trust Inheritance Process

  1. Trust Administration: Successor trustee obtains death certificate, lodges will, and issues California Probate Code § 16061.7 notice to beneficiaries/heirs.
  2. Date-of-Death Valuation: Hire certified appraiser for Property A FMV as of Date of Death (sets IRC § 1014 basis).
  3. Affidavit of Death of Trustee: Record Affidavit + Preliminary Change of Ownership Report (PCOR) with Santa Clara County Clerk-Recorder.
  4. Execute Chosen Path: Either (a) list & sell in same tax year, (b) move in within 1 year & file BOE-19-P, or (c) lease at market rent ($5,800/mo) and set up 27.5-yr depreciation schedule.
