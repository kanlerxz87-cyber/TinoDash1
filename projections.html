Reinvestment risk beside the headline: the phased path is not “free yield.” If the Fed cuts 300 bp before your tranches mature, you reinvest at ~1.4% for the remainder. The ladder’s job is crash protection, not yield maximization — that is why the window is 24–36 months, not 10 years. Treasury interest is ordinary federal income, exempt from CA/local tax (IRC §103) — a real after-tax edge vs dividends.
