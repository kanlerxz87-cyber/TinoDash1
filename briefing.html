2. Estate Tax (Federal & CA)

  • 2026 Federal Exemption: $15,000,000 individual / $30,000,000 married (PL 119-21, 40% top rate).
  • CA Estate Tax: $0.
  • Action: Confirm portability election (Form 706) & prior taxable gifts (>$19,000/yr).

3. Income-Tax Basis (IRC § 1014)

  • Step-Up at Death: Property A receives 100% FMV step-up at Date of Death.
  • Same-Year Sale: $0 capital gains if sold near DOD value.
  • CA Form 540: Post-death gains taxed as ordinary income up to 12.3% + 1% BHST >$1M (13.3%).

4. Property Tax (Prop 19)

  • 2025–2027 Cap: $1,044,586 (indexed biennially).
  • Requirement: Heir must occupy Property A as primary residence within 1 year & file BOE-19-P within 3 years.
  • If Rented: $0 exclusion; full FMV reassessment.

5. Key Questions for Counsel & Liquidity Plan

  1. Portability & Prior Gifts: Were any Form 709 gift returns filed, and is DSUE portability available from a predeceased spouse?

  3. 1031 Exchange vs. Same-Year Sale: Confirm whether a same-year sale (capturing § 1014 step-up) is strictly superior to holding and later doing a 1031 exchange.
  4. Investment Policy Statement (IPS): Draft IPS for sale proceeds (e.g., 24–36 mo 3M–2Y Treasury ladder vs. immediate equity allocation) before funds arrive.
