1 · The situation in four lines

  • Property A — Cupertino 95014, est. $3,100,000 (Nov 2026). Held in a trust; passes to the heir at the decedent's death, expected 2040–2050.
  • Property B — San Francisco 94122, est. $1,800,000. Already owned by the heir. Three units, two rented at ~$7,150/mo, one owner-occupiable.
  • The estate may exceed the federal exclusion ($15,000,000 in 2026; California has no estate or inheritance tax).

4 · Decisions we need from you

  1. Portability — has it been used? Were there prior taxable gifts? (Form 709 history)

  5. Trust — does the instrument require a sale to divide the estate?
  6. Occupancy — confirm the Prop 19 filing sequence and the evidence of occupancy you will keep.
