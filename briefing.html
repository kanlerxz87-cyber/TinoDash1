Attorney & CPA Briefing Sheet — Inheritance of Property A

Placeholders only: Property A = inherited Cupertino home (ZIP 95014, est. ≈ $3,100,000 Nov 2026, factored base ≈ $550,540). Property B = heir’s existing SF Outer Sunset 3-unit rental (ZIP 94122), ≈ $1,800,000 — context only. No personal data is included or implied; all figures rounded to the nearest $10. Expected transfer (trust distribution): ≈ 2040–2050. This sheet lists what we need from counsel; it is not advice and creates no engagement.

1) The decision on the table

Live in A / rent A out / sell A at (or near) the date of death and reinvest — plus how B is held (keep rental + owner unit; or sell). The family wants written triggers agreed in advance (pre-commitment) so the outcome is not reverse-engineered from a projection.

2) For the attorney

  • Confirm trust terms: distribution trigger, discretion, successor trustee powers of sale, in terrorem clauses, and whether A is specifically devised.
  • Confirm gross estate vs $15,000,000 federal basic exclusion (2026; OBBBA §70106; IRC §2010) — $30,000,000 couple with portability (Form 706); indexed from 2027 (≈ $15,480,000 in 2027). Prior taxable gifts (Form 709) count against it.
  • Advise on portability election (Form 706 within 9 months + 6-month extension; ask about late-election relief per Form 706 instructions) even if no tax is due.
  • Confirm Prop 19 (RTC §63.2) sequence: homeowners’ exemption claim (BOE-266) ≤ 1 year from transfer; parent-child exclusion claim (BOE-19-P) ≤ 3 years, before any third-party transfer; require heir occupancy + written intent letter; assess partial-reassessment mechanics (taxable value = FMV − allowance; $1,044,586 for 2/16/2025–2/15/2027 transfers, biennially adjusted).
  • Advise on IRC §121 (2-of-5-year ownership & use; $250,000 single / $500,000 MFJ exclusion) for the “live in A, then sell” path.
  • Advise whether a like-kind exchange (IRC §1031) is wanted for any investment disposition (not available for a primary residence; must be held for investment; strict 45/180-day clocks).
  • Draft/confirm escrow, entity, and title mechanics for sale at death (who signs), supplemental assessment protest window, and any debt payoff/HELOC on A or B.
  • Confirm estate liquidity: if estate may exceed the exclusion (or cash is needed for taxes/debt/equalization), options incl. IRC §6166 installment for closely-held business (likely N/A — real estate), §2032A special-use (N/A residential), life insurance, or sale protocol.

3) For the CPA / tax adviser

  • Model sale in the same tax year as death: step-up in basis (IRC §1014) ⇒ pre-death appreciation ≈ $0 federal/CA gain; confirm community-property double step-up if applicable; confirm holding period long-term either way.
  • Compute CA tax: ordinary rates (top 12.3%) + 1% Behavioral Health Services Tax (f/k/a Mental Health Services Tax) on CA taxable income > $1,000,000 — Form 540 line for BHSA, whole-dollar computation; note CA taxes capital gains as ordinary income (no preferential LTCG rate).
  • Federal LTCG 2026 (Rev. Proc. 2025-32): 0% ≤ $49,450 single / $98,900 MFJ; 15% ≤ $545,500 single / $613,700 MFJ; 20% above; +3.8% NIIT (MAGI > $200,000 single / $250,000 MFJ).
  • Confirm unrecaptured §1250 gain treatment (up to 25%) on post-death depreciation only — basis resets at death, so pre-death depreciation is not recaptured for the heir.
  • Re-run CA BHSA sensitivity: 1% of CA taxable income over $1,000,000 (e.g., $5,000,000 over → $50,000) — built into Tax Worksheet; federal NIIT on the same excess.
  • Track 2040–2050 horizon law changes: estate basic exclusion indexation (from 2027), AB 1482 sunset 2030-01-01, SF Prop H from 2027-07-01 (≈ $250/yr multi-unit parcel, inflation-adjusted; possible $65/unit pass-through), rent-control coverage of B, Prop 19 allowance re-index every 2 years.
  • Confirm treatment of Treasury interest on the phased path (federal ordinary income; exempt from CA income tax), and capital-loss harvesting plan if a black-swan entry occurs.

4) Documents we will bring

A’s tax bills (2014–2026) & any TRA code; trust + pour-over will; B’s leases & CO; rent roll ($7,150/mo, 2 of 3 units; water $330–$400/mo; electric $30–$70/mo); A’s utility history (water $100–$150/mo; solar credits ≈ $10–$20/mo); current insurance declarations (to be re-quoted); written date-of-death appraisal when available; portfolio statements; list of lifetime taxable gifts.

5) Decision deadlines (see full Timeline)

Date of death + 9 mo: Form 706 (if required) / portability. + 1 yr: BOE-266 (Prop 19, occupancy paths only). + 3 yrs: BOE-19-P. Sales at death: no income-tax step-up deadline, but escrow/supplemental-assessment timing matters. SF Prop H begins 2027-07-01. AB 1482 sunset 2030-01-01. Re-run model annually.

One-line ask: “Before we see the appraisal or any projection, tell us which path each of you would advise and precisely what would change your advice — then we will hold you to it in the pre-commitment letter.”
