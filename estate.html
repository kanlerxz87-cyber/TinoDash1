Separate estate tax, income-tax basis, and property-tax assessment — they are three different laws

Do not conflate these — each has its own threshold, timing, and form

ConceptWhat it isThreshold / rate (current law, 2026)Source
Federal estate tax
(on total estate at death)		Tax on decedent’s taxable estate (everything owned + prior gifts) above federal exclusion. 18–40% progressive. Paid by estate, not heir, within 9 mo of death (Form 706).2024 exclusion $13.61M / person; 2025 ~$13.99M; 2026 scheduled to sunset to ~$6.8–7M (pre-TCJA, indexed) unless Congress extends. Portability lets surviving spouse use deceased spouse’s unused exclusion (DSUE) if 706 filed. Prior taxable gifts count.IRS Estate Tax · Form 706 Instructions · TCJA sunset (26 USC 2010)
Income-tax basis (step-up)
(on heir’s future capital gain)		Heir’s basis in inherited Property A = FMV at death (or alternate valuation 6 mo later). This wipes pre-death gain. If heir sells same year, taxable gain ≈ $0. Not the same as property-tax assessment.IRC §1014. Basis = DOD value — need licensed appraisal at DOD. If estate tax return filed, value on Form 706 controls basis (consistency rule).IRS Pub 551 — Basis · 26 USC §1014 · Form 706
Property-tax assessment
(annual CA property tax)		CA assessed value for annual 1%+levy property tax. Capped at +2% (Prop 13). At inheritance, resets to market unless Prop 19 principal-residence exclusion applies. Independent of estate tax and basis.1% base + voter-approved debt (~0.16–0.25% in Cupertino) — see TRA. Prop 19 allowance ~$1M+ indexed if heir occupies. BOE-19-P within 1 yr.BOE Prop 13 FAQ · BOE Prop 19 · SCC Assessor
Example: Estate worth $15M in 2026 with $14M exclusion → ~$1M taxable at 40% = $400k estate tax. Heir’s basis in A is still FMV (~$3.1M) — estate tax and basis are separate calculations. Property-tax assessed value may be $550k or $3.1M depending on Prop 19 — a third separate number. All sources

California estate / inheritance tax
$0 — CA has none
CA has no state estate/inheritance tax (only federal). Source: FTB Estates & Trusts · BOE — prop-tax is separate.
CA local inheritance nuance
Property tax ≠ inheritance tax
Inheritance via trust can still trigger Prop 19 reassessment — file BOE-502-A. No CA inheritance tax return required.
Prepare an estate liquidity plan if the estate may exceed the federal exclusion. Estate tax is due in cash within 9 mo — illiquid estates (house-heavy) may need life insurance, HELOC, or planned sale. Ask now about portability (was Form 706 filed for first spouse’s DSUE?) and prior taxable gifts (Form 709) — both reduce available exclusion. See deadlines and checklist.
