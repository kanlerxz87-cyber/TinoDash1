Reinvestment Risk Notice: Any staging/phased-investing headline carries reinvestment & inflation-drag risk. If equities rally +15%–25% during the 24–36 month Treasury window, or if Treasury yields drop at maturity, phased investing trails lump-sum investing. Conversely, in a 1929 or 2008 crash at inheritance, staging preserves principal and buys equities at 35%–50% discounts.

Phased-Investing Policy (24–36 Month Treasury Ladder)

  • Months 0–12: 100% in 3M–12M U.S. Treasury Bills (state-tax-exempt in California; ~4.0%–4.5% nominal yield).
  • Months 13–24: Phase 33% into broad-market equities (e.g., S&P 500 / Total Market); keep 67% in 6M–2Y Treasuries.
  • Months 25–36: Phase remaining 67% in two equal tranches (Month 24 and Month 36), or accelerate if S&P 500 drops >20% from peak.
