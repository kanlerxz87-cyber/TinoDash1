Dedicated Black-Swan section — real economic conditions, not smooth 50-year lines

Why growth & inflation are never consistent — and why 1929/2008 entry matters

Baseline projections often draw a straight 6.5% stock line for 50 years. Real history never did: 1929–1932 (–86%), 1973–74 (–48%), 2000–02 (–49%), 2008 (–57%), 2020 (–34%), 2022 (–25%) — inflation 13% in 1979–80, deflation in 2009. If you inherit and invest the $3M sale proceeds the day before a crash, lump-sum entry can underperform housing for a decade. The phased path exists to test whether parking proceeds in 3M–2Y Treasuries for 24–36 months then phasing into stocks softens that risk — and at what reinvestment cost.

Reinvestment risk — beside the headline: Every month you stay in Treasuries hoping to avoid a crash, you risk reinvesting later at lower yields/stock prices if the crash never comes. There is no free hedge. The only way to make “phased” disciplined is a written IPS with a pre-committed DCA schedule — otherwise phasing becomes market timing.

Phased-investing path (official terms)

Park sale proceeds in 3M–2Y Treasuries for 24–36 months after inheritance before phasing into stocks

  1. Day 1 (sale settles): Proceeds → 3M T-Bill ladder + 6M/1Y/2Y mix (ladder so something matures monthly). Current 2Y ~4.0–4.5% (check Treasury at inheritance).
  2. Months 1–36: Collect interest (fed-taxable, CA-exempt for direct Treasuries). Do not extend duration to chase yield unless IPS says so.
  3. Months 13–24 (or 25–36): Begin DCA: 1/12 of Treasury pool → 70/30 stocks/bonds each month over 12 months. Rebalance quarterly.
  4. After phased: Glide to long-term allocation (e.g., 80/20 → 60/40 by retirement). Document in IPS.

CA exempts direct Treasury interest from state tax — a 0.5–1.0% edge vs CDs. Source: FTB 540 Booklet — Treasury interest. Ladder example: 4 × $750k rungs at 3M/6M/1Y/2Y.

When phased helps: Household is leaning Sell for fundamental reasons (liquidity, Prop 19 unavailable, high carry) but fears immediate market top. Phasing lets them sell now with a pre-committed, unemotional entry — 12 equal monthly buys regardless of headlines.
When phased hurts: Household uses “wait in Treasuries” as open-ended delay. If yields fall 4.5%→2.5%, $3M loses $60k/yr income; if stocks rise while waiting, DCA buys higher. Fix the DCA calendar in the IPS before the sale.

Lesson: 50-yr averages hide decade-long regimes. Use ranges; stress with 1970s stagflation (housing wins), 1990s equity boom (stocks win), 2000s crash (housing wins early, stocks win later), and run what-ifs annually.

Review extreme scenarios annually — documented IPS trigger

Annual check: Are we in a 1929/2008 regime? What does the IPS say to do?

  • Trigger: If S&P 500 drawdown >20% at inheritance, extend Treasury phase to 36 mo and widen DCA to 18 mo — do not pause.
  • Trigger: If 10Y yield <3% when Treasuries mature, accept lower reinvestment income per plan — do not chase yield with long duration.
  • Trigger: If Cupertino rent falls >10% while vacancy >7%, re-price Rent path and consider selling sooner.

Write these triggers in IPS before money arrives — not after headlines arrive. Legislation tracker also rechecked quarterly.
