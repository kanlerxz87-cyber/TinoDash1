Show the deadlines as a timeline

From date of death to annual re-checks — what must be filed, when, and what happens if you miss it

All deadlines count from date of death (DOD). This timeline covers the inheritance-by-trust path for Property A (Cupertino) + ongoing stewardship of Property B (SF). Verify each year — laws change. Enacted vs Proposed distinction per tracker.

Immediately — secure & occupy decision

Day 1–14: Death certificate, trustee affidavit, secure property, decide preliminary occupancy

Obtain certified death certificates (10–15 copies). Successor trustee records Affidavit of Death of Trustee (CA). Change locks, forward mail, notify insurer. Do not transfer title to personal name until attorney reviews trust distribution language — Prop 19 eligibility can depend on who the “transferor” was at death. Source: SCC Change in Ownership · CA Probate Code §15600

DOD valuation — obtain a written valuation at date of death

Within 30–60 days: Licensed appraisal of Property A at DOD (written, defensible)

Order retrospective appraisal effective DOD from CA-licensed appraiser (not Zestimate). This sets your step-up basis (IRC §1014) and supports both estate tax (Form 706) and future capital-gain reporting. Cost ~$500–$900 in Bay Area (rounded $10). If you sell same-year, this appraisal wipes gain — get it before deciding. Source: IRS Pub 551 · CA BREA license verification

Prop 19 + change-in-ownership filing

Within 1 year of transfer: Preliminary Change in Ownership Report (BOE-502-A) + Prop 19 Parent-Child Exclusion Claim

Hard deadline: 1 year. If heir will make A their principal residence long-term, file Homeowners’ Exemption (BOE-266) + Prop 19 claim (BOE-19-P or county equivalent) within 1 yr — and actually occupy. Miss it = assessed value resets to DOD market ($3.1M in example) permanently. Allowance (~$1M+ indexed) adjusts annually. Source: BOE Prop 19 official · SCC Prop 19 filing

Same-year sale window

If selling: aim to close in same tax year as inheritance

Same-year sale = basis step-up erases pre-death gain; only sale-date market move taxed. If death is late in tax year (e.g., Oct 2040), you have weeks to decide — pre-obtain appraisal and title, line up agent, and document IPS choice. If you miss the tax year, yr+1 appreciation becomes taxable (still mitigated by §121 if you lived 2 yr). Source: IRS Pub 551

Before selling: Decide if 1031 exchange wanted — QI must be engaged before sale closes; personal-residence property not eligible. IRS 1031 · Ask attorney.
Federal estate tax (if applicable)

9 months after DOD: Form 706 due (6-mo extension available via 4768)

Due 9 mo after DOD; extension to 15 mo for filing, but tax payment still due at 9 mo unless §6161 extension. Portability election (DSUE) requires timely 706 even if no tax due. Prior taxable gifts (Form 709) reduce exclusion — gather now. Source: Form 706 instructions · IRS Estate Tax

Property-tax assessment appeals

July 2 – Sept 15 (SCC) / Sept 1 cutoff (SF): Assessment Appeal if you dispute new assessed value

If assessed value after inheritance seems wrong, file Assessment Appeal Application with county Assessment Appeals Board within the window. Separate from Prop 19 claim. Source: SCC Assessment Appeals · SF Assessment Appeals

§121 exclusion clock

2 of last 5 years: Own + use as principal residence to capture $250k/$500k exclusion on later sale

If you occupy A, the 2-yr clock starts at move-in. Do not rent A during the 2-yr exclusion qualification period if you plan to use §121 — you can qualify even if you rent briefly after, as long as 2 of last 5 conditions met at sale. Document move-in (DMV, voter, utility). Source: IRS Pub 523 · 26 USC §121

Annual stewardship

Every year: Re-run model, verify leases & local rules, review extreme scenarios, recheck legislation

Update: home values, rents, rates, Prop 19 indexed allowance, federal exclusion (re-check Rev. Proc.), SF Rent Board rules, insurance, capital plan spend. Verify B’s leases, SF rent-control caps (currently 60% of CPI, max 7% — verify annually), condo/local rules. Review black-swan triggers in IPS. Source: SF Rent Board · SF Transfer Tax · Legislation tracker
