Plan ahead

From preparation to transfer

Before transfer · now and annuallyPrivately inventory assets, liabilities, taxable gifts and portability; agree in writing on decision triggers; commission rent estimates, insurance quotes and five-year capital plans. Official source ↗
At death · confirm authorityNotify trustee and advisers; secure assets, records, insurance and cash. Obtain written date-of-death valuation. Official source ↗
Within one year · if eligibleThe transferee must move in within one year and continue to occupy. Claim the homeowners’ or disabled veterans’ exemption within one year for relief from transfer; a later claim can provide prospective relief. Confirm all filing deadlines with the assessor. Official source ↗
Estate filing and liquidityAsk the executor about Form 706, portability and payment deadlines and any extension; do not assume the return is unnecessary. Official source ↗
Before any saleCompare same-year sale against alternatives, obtain closing estimates and decide whether an exchange is appropriate before disposition. Official source ↗
Every year thereafterUpdate laws, leases, insurance, capital budgets, inflation and stress tests; record decision, date, rationale and next review privately. Official source ↗
